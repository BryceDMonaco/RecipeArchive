## Ingredients

- 2 cups sugar
- 3 eggs
- 1 cup vegetable oil
- 2 cups grated zucchini
- 3 cups flour
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 1 tsp ginger (do not add if making chocolate bread)
- 1 tsp cinnamon (do not add if making chocolate bread)
- 1 tsp baking soda
- 1 tsp baking powder
- 1 tsp salt
- 1/4 cup milk
- 1 cup chocolate chips (optional, add more to preference)
- 1 cup cocoa powder (optional, see notes)
- 1 cup espresso powder or instant coffee (optional, see notes)

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 325°F.
2. Mix sugar, oil, and eggs.
3. Add zucchini to the mix.
4. Add the remaining ingredients.
5. Pour batter into two 9x5 loaf pans and bake for one hour.

## Notes

Makes two loaves.

### To Make Chocolate Zucchini Bread:
- Do not add ginger and cinnamon
- Add 1 cup cocoa powder and 1 tsp espresso powder or instant coffee
- Add chocolate chips if not already
