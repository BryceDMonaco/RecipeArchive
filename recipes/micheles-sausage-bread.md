## Ingredients

- 1 can refrigerated French bread dough
- 1 lb ground pork sausage (mild or spicy)
- 2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 350°F and line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
2. Brown the sausage in a large skillet over medium-high heat, breaking it into small pieces, for about 10 minutes or until no longer pink. Drain on paper towels.
3. Find the seam of the French bread dough and unroll it onto the parchment-lined baking sheet, forming a rectangle.
4. Spread the cooked sausage evenly over the dough, then sprinkle the mozzarella over the sausage.
5. Starting from the long side, roll the dough up tightly into a log and pinch the ends to seal.
6. Place the log seam side down and cut a few shallow slits across the top.
7. Bake for about 30 minutes, until golden brown and crusty.
8. Let rest for 5 to 10 minutes, then slice into 1 to 1 1/2 inch pieces and serve.
